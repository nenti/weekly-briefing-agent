# The "About me" prompt

Paste this into Claude (or Claude Code) before the workshop. It interviews you and
writes a short, reusable profile of who you are, what drives you, and how you work.
Save the result as `me.md` and bring it — it's the first thing your agent will read.

Keep it afterwards: this profile is reusable. Drop it into *any* AI tool to set context
about yourself in one paste. The depth (what you value, what drives you) is what makes
an email actually sound like you, not like an assistant.

---

```
You're going to help me write a short "About me" profile - a reusable context block I
can paste into any AI so it instantly knows who it's working for and what I care about.

Interview me first. Ask these one at a time, and wait for my answer before the next:

1. What's your role, and what does a typical work week look like?
2. What are the 3 things you most want to stop doing by hand?
3. What drives you - what are you really trying to build or achieve, and what do you
   believe about how it should be done?
4. What do you value most in how you deal with people? Anything an email in your name
   should always do, or never do?
5. Who do you work with most - team, clients, partners?
6. How do you like to sound when you write - formal, warm, short? Which languages?

When I've answered, write my profile as a clean markdown block under a "# About me"
heading: my role, what drives me and what I value, what matters day to day, my voice,
and any key context. Keep it tight but with real depth - the kind of context that makes
an email actually sound like me. Then remind me to save it as me.md and bring it to the
workshop.
```

---

**Note for the build:** question 2 ("3 things you most want to stop doing by hand")
doubles as the automation ideas each person brings to share with the group.
